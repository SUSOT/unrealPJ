# Showcase1 horror audio

Footsteps, relay click, cutlery, and the foley grains in the chair-drag composite are derived from
[Kenney Impact Sounds 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Downloaded from the official Kenney site on 2026-09-04. No attribution is required;
credit is retained here for provenance. The original license is `Kenney-License.txt`.

`SW_Loop_RoomTone` is original procedural electrical/ventilation audio generated by
`Scripts/prepare_showcase_horror_audio.py`. No external music or voice is used.
All eight files are mono 44.1 kHz PCM16. Peaks and RMS are recorded in `Audio/audio_metrics.json`.
Runtime volume is independently adjustable on the ShowcaseLoopDirector in Showcase1.

To regenerate: obtain Kenney's free ZIP into `Saved/ShowcaseExpansion/AudioSource`,
extract to `KenneyImpact`, then run the preparation script with numpy and soundfile.
`Scripts/prepare_showcase_reverse_audio.py` creates the additional 1.5-second cutlery cue
from `impactPlate_light_001`, `impactPlate_light_003`, and `impactMetal_light_002` in that same CC0 pack.
